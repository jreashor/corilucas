Photos and artwork added to the site must be CC0 or public domain, so no attribution, permission or licence conditions follow them. Sources whose licences merely allow free use, or paid stock sites, are not used.

The hero background photo (`public/images/leaf-drop.jpg`) is a CC0 image taken from Wikimedia Commons ("Green Leaf With Water Drop"). Commons records the CC0 status as the uploader's claim, so check the file's own page for the licence before adding an image.
