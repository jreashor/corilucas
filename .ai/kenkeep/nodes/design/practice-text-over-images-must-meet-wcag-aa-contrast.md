Text laid over a photograph must meet WCAG AA contrast: at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text. Measure it against the brightest part of the photo, not its average, because water-drop highlights and similar spots set the worst case.

Reach the ratio by strengthening the overlay between photo and text (a darker green at high opacity) and by using near-full-opacity text, rather than leaving a faint overlay and hoping the text reads. The hero section follows this: a dark sage overlay at 90% opacity over the photo, with white text at 90% or higher.

The rule exists because a lighter overlay left the hero paragraph and eyebrow line hard to read and failing accessibility checks.
